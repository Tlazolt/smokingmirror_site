Word two was compass. Correct.

Four travelers reached the Waystation one at a time: Ada, Bo, Cy and Dee. Each carried one thing: a bell, a map, a rope or an anchor. No two carried the same thing.

  • Bo arrived before Ada and after Dee.
  • Cy arrived last.
  • The first to arrive carried the bell.
  • Ada carried the map.
  • The rope did not arrive last.
